Use

While DNC is 94
Echad RingEXP +150%

Equip it and choose Use, then camp Rala, Ceizak, or Yahse. One charge, ready again after 2 hours. The bonus ends at 30,000 extra EXP or after 12 hours. It equips at level 1 and refills on its own, so this is the ring that stays on the 94 to 99 climb.

Caliber Ringbuy · 480 hallmarks

Same 30,000 cap and 12 hours as Echad, with three charges ready again after 15 minutes. Buy from Gorpa-Masorpa in Mhaura (G-9) for 480 hallmarks (one per month). The charges never refill. Until then, bridge with Emperor Band while Echad is on its 2-hour recharge.

Emperor BandEXP +50% · bridge

The bridge when Echad is recharging and Caliber is not available. Three charges, each ready again after 15 minutes, same 30,000 cap and 12 hours. Refill a spent charge for 200 conquest points. Keep Empress and Chariot out of the bag: this is the only conquest EXP band to hold.

Kupofried+20% · stacks

Summon with //tru exp on EXP camps. The aura is +20% EXP and stacks with the ring. The rings do not stack with each other: each one grants Dedication, and using a second ring replaces the bonus that is already up.

Next · INT210 to 60

Assign on THF 99, in a Mog House or Mog Garden: Main Menu → Status → Alter Ego Points. Points cannot be moved once placed. INT is magic damage for Shantotto II. Ranks 1–60 cost 210. New points go here until INT is 60/60.

ThenMND → Magic Skill

MND is cures and enfeebles. Magic Skill is what lets Koru-Moru land Slow and Paralyze instead of refusing to cast them. Each is another 210 to reach 60. STR, DEX, and Combat Skill wait: the Dancer is the one doing the killing.

After DNC hits 99
Endorsement RingCP +200%

The strongest capacity ring, once Deeds of Heroism reach 30. The Validator trades that tier for this ring. One charge, ready again after 2 hours, and the bonus ends at 30,000 extra CP or 12 hours. It equips at level 99. Deeds are at 20, so this waits until the next tier.

Facility RingCP +150% · use first

Same +150% / 30,000 CP / 12 hours as Trizek, with three charges ready again after 15 minutes. After DNC hits 99, burn Facility charges before Trizek. The charges never refill; buy the next one from Gorpa-Masorpa for 800 hallmarks when this one is spent.

Trizek RingCP +150%

One charge, ready again after 2 hours, same 30,000 CP cap and 12 hours. It refills on its own. Use it when Facility is empty or recharging between Ambuscade buys.

Capacity RingCP +50%

Use a charge when the +150% or +200% ring is down. Each charge is ready again after 15 minutes, and the cap is still 30,000. A spare is 5,000 sparks at Rolandienne only if you want to share the one you wear. Skip Vocation Ring: its 12,000 CP cap empties faster on these camps.

Aptitude Mantlebuy at 99

AH level 99 back piece. The +1 is +30%. Wear it when the monster dies. This stacks with Facility/Trizek/Capacity and with Kupofried's +20% CP aura. Stop using Echad and Emperor once the job is 99.

Before it caps or expires
Sparks76,771

Cap is 99,999, and sparks past that disappear. At Rolandienne in Southern San d'Oria (G-10), buy Acheron Shield from the level 71–98 list (2,755 sparks) and sell the shield to a vendor. That is the gil dump, and the weekly exchange limit (100,000) covers this pile. DNC gear is covered, so do not buy more of it with sparks. A second Capacity Ring is 5,000 sparks, only if you want a spare. Vendor dagger enchiridions; they are a worse sparks purchase than the shield.

Unity Accolades · Apururu66,555

Same 99,999 cap. Apururu only boosts that unity's Wanted NMs. Spend the points at any Unity NPC, such as Urbiolaine in Southern San d'Oria. Keep a few thousand for Wanted warps while you run missions. Buy Prize Powder with the rest (10 accolades each) and vendor it. An SP Gobbie Key costs 15,000. Spin the two keys when a free inventory slot is open.

Login pointsOct open

October points accrue from October 14 through November 7; Greeter Moogle exchange runs through November 9 (700 carry into the next month). This month's cipher shop is complete. Leftover points: Track Pants (10 each; buy two to Synergy into +1 movement speed) if you still want them, otherwise hold under the 700 carry. Skip the Scientist's Board unless you want the furnishing.

Copper A.M.A.N. vouchers43 · hold

Store them at a Records of Eminence NPC (up to 999) and convert only when a harder-to-farm currency is immediately needed. Do not burn 15–23 on Allied Notes for an Allied Ring: Echad and Emperor already cover the remaining DNC levels, and Caliber is the better hallmark spend. Resistance Credits, Obsidian Fragments, or starter Escha Silt are better emergency uses later.

From October 16 through November 9, a coalition assignment triples the bayld it pays, and a reive roughly doubles bayld and experience up to the usual cap on that reive.

Mog Pell red / silver8 / 4

A silver pell is a JSE cape or the Abdhaljs materials for one, so those four wait until DNC is 99 and you pick the cape.

Abdhaljs seal / nugget · hallmarks · gallantry3 / 1 · 0 · 0

From October 16 through November 9, gallantry from one normal or intense run each day is doubled, and that bonus stacks with a seal.
