How to clear Easy
Ambu Daily3 Wekufe

In Records of Eminence, keep Ambuscade Primer Vol. 2. It wants 3 undead that give experience. The Volume One primer is the Dullahan ticket, and that fight starts when the gearset reads 119.


Home Point warp to Fei'Yin #1. You land at (K-8) on map 1, beside the Qu'Bia Arena door.

If that crystal is missing from the list, zone in from Beaucedine Glacier and walk east to the two dolls and the cermet gate, then click the crystal there. From the crystal, the four small rooms are (K-8), (J-8), (I-9), and (J-10).

Call trusts with //tru and kill 3 Wekufe. They are ghosts and give experience at level 99.


When the objective completes, the primer is a temporary key item. Go to Mhaura (G-9) and examine the Ambuscade Tome beside Gorpa-Masorpa. Choose Regular Ambuscade, then Easy, and enter on Thief.The gearset should still read at least 114.

A win spends the primer, so kill 3 more Wekufe before the next entry.

Trusts · //trudefault

August, Apururu, Koru-Moru, Qultada, Shantotto II. Silence works on Lancer Jack. He calls a skeleton, a hound, and a ghost. Kill him. His trick move silences you, terrifies you, and locks your abilities.

Dullahan ticket, then Mhaura1 Dullahan

Ready when the Thief set reads item level 119. Volume 1 Very Easy is the first Dullahan battle, and it asks for 119. Easy asks for 124, Normal for 129, Difficult for 134, and Very Difficult for 139. At 119, choose Very Easy. The set reads 117, so this waits.


Set Ambuscade Primer Vol. 1 (Daily) when you are about to spend it. You can hold one Volume One primer, and a win is what spends it. The daily wants 1 Dullahan. Warp to Ceizak Bivouac #1 (H-9). Run south into Sih Gates at G-11. Click the rock.

Another Volume 1 run the same day uses Ambuscade Primer Vol. 1. That objective wants 3 Unrelenting Dullahans at the same I-7 camp. The daily resets at Japanese midnight.

Use

While Dancer is 94
Echad RingEXP +150%

Put it on and choose Use, then camp Rala, Ceizak, or Yahse. One charge, ready again after 2 hours. The bonus stops at 30,000 extra experience or after 12 hours. It can be worn at level 1 and refills by itself, so this is the ring for the climb from 94 to 99.

Caliber Ringbuy · 480 hallmarks

Same 30,000 cap and 12 hours as Echad. Three charges, each ready again after 15 minutes. Buy it from Gorpa-Masorpa in Mhaura (G-9) for 480 hallmarks, once a month. The charges never come back. Until you buy it, use Emperor Band while Echad is recharging.

Emperor BandEXP +50% · fill-in

Use this while Echad is recharging and you do not have Caliber yet. Three charges, each ready again after 15 minutes. Same 30,000 cap and 12 hours. Refill a used charge for 200 conquest points. Leave Empress Band and Chariot Band alone. This is the only conquest experience band to keep.

Kupofried+20% · stacks

Summon with //tru exp on experience camps. The aura is +20% experience and adds to the ring. The rings do not add to each other. Using a second ring replaces the bonus that is already on.

Trust parties & controls
Default · //trubalanced

August, Apururu (UC), Koru-Moru, Qultada, Shantotto II. Apururu is the healer. Use this for normal play, and when the camp party needs more healing.

EXP · //tru expKupofried +20%

August, Koru-Moru, Qultada, Kupofried, Shantotto II. Use this on Rala, Ceizak, or Yahse so the +20% aura adds to Echad or Emperor. Switch to the default party if waltzes are not enough healing.

Camps · //tru campsSakura regen

August, Koru-Moru, Qultada, Sakura, Shantotto II. Same damage party, with Sakura instead of Kupofried, when you want healing over time instead of the experience aura.

Raise · //tru raiserecovery

August, Apururu (UC), Koru-Moru, Qultada, Ferreous Coffin. Use this on risky fights, when a trust that can Raise is worth giving up Shantotto II.

Boss · //tru bosssurvival

Amchuchu, Apururu (UC), King of Hearts, Koru-Moru, Shantotto II. Amchuchu holds hate while the healer and support trusts keep you up.

GearSwap key overlayThief · Dancer · Ninja

Hold Ctrl, Alt, Ctrl+Shift, Alt+Shift, or Ctrl+Alt to see that row. GearSwap reloads the labels once you are in the game. //kl unlock lets you move the heading, the actions, and the descriptions. //kl lock saves them.

Pass drops · /ll junkauto

LightLuggage does not load by itself. Each session, type load ll, then /ll junk. Trash passes as it drops and never takes a slot. Crystals, seals, Ninja tools, seeds, scrolls, and copper or iron ore stay.

Sell that list · //sellnpc junkthen open a shop

SellNPC loads with Windower. Run the command, move the stacks into your main inventory, then open any city shop. Matching stacks sell. Prize powder and copper or iron ore have their own commands: //sellnpc powder and //sellnpc ore.

Next · INT210 to 60

Set this on Thief 99, in a Mog House or Mog Garden. Open Main Menu, then Status, then Alter Ego Points. You cannot move points after you place them. INT is magic damage for Shantotto II. Ranks 1 through 60 cost 210 points. Put new points here until INT is 60/60.

ThenMND → Magic Skill

MND makes cures and enfeebles stronger. Magic Skill lets Koru-Moru land Slow and Paralyze. Each one is another 210 points to reach 60. Leave STR, DEX, and Combat Skill for later. The Dancer is the one doing the killing.

After Dancer hits 99
Endorsement RingCP +200%

The strongest Capacity Point ring, once Deeds of Heroism reach 30. The reward NPC trades that tier for this ring. One charge, ready again after 2 hours. The bonus stops at 30,000 extra Capacity Points or after 12 hours. It can be worn at level 99. Deeds are at 20, so this waits for the next tier.

Facility RingCP +150% · use first

Same +150%, 30,000 Capacity Points, and 12 hours as Trizek, with three charges ready again after 15 minutes. After Dancer hits 99, use Facility charges before Trizek. The charges never come back. Buy the next one from Gorpa-Masorpa for 800 hallmarks when this one is used up.

Trizek RingCP +150%

One charge, ready again after 2 hours. Same 30,000 Capacity Point cap and 12 hours. It refills by itself. Use it when Facility is empty, or still recharging, between Ambuscade buys.

Capacity RingCP +50%

Use a charge when the +150% or +200% ring is down. Each charge is ready again after 15 minutes. The cap is still 30,000. A spare is 5,000 sparks at Rolandienne, only if you want a second one. Leave Vocation Ring alone. Its 12,000 Capacity Point cap runs out faster on these camps.

Aptitude Mantlebuy at 99

Buy this level 99 back piece from the auction house. The +1 version is +30%. Wear it when the monster dies. It adds to Facility, Trizek, Capacity, and Kupofried's +20% aura. Stop using Echad and Emperor once the job is 99.
